ChefLoop

A recipe app for one cook or a whole household, each person with their own Apple Account. Save recipes, plan the week, build the shopping list, and cook step by step, all on your iPhone.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your recipes live on your phone and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see them.

What it does

  • Bring recipes in from a web link, a photo, pasted text, or a recipe file made with an AI assistant.
  • Scale servings and switch between metric and imperial.
  • Cooking mode: big text, hands-free voice commands, and timers that ring even when your phone is locked or silent.
  • Meal plan, shopping list grouped by aisle, and a pantry of staples.
  • Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts.
  • Optional on-device Apple Intelligence for tidying imported recipes. Nothing leaves your phone.
