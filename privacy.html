Privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-08

In short: ChefLoop collects nothing. We run no servers and have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We cannot see your data and we do not sell or share it. If you choose to share a household with people you live with, that sharing happens through Apple's iCloud, not through us.

What ChefLoop stores

Your recipes, original recipe text, shopping lists, meal plan, pantry, notes and your own photos of finished dishes. This stays on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for ChefLoop, it is also kept in your own private iCloud space so your devices stay in sync. That storage belongs to you and is run by Apple under Apple's privacy terms. The developer has no access to it.

Small settings (units, theme, saved filters, running timers), your favourites and your choice of unit for an ingredient are stored on your device only.

Sharing a household (optional)

You can start a household and invite the people you live with, each with their own Apple Account. When you do, your recipes, meal plan, shopping list and pantry are shared with them through iCloud's sharing feature (Apple's CloudKit). You start it and you choose who is invited. The shared data lives in the household owner's iCloud space, which counts against the owner's iCloud storage, and each person's iPhone keeps a copy. ChefLoop has no server of its own: we do not receive, store or see the household's data, the invitations, or who is in a household. Anyone in the household can see and change what is shared. Anyone can leave at any time and can keep a copy of the recipes. The owner can stop sharing at any time. Apple handles invitations and sharing under Apple's privacy terms.

Purchases

ChefLoop Plus (a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock) is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and Family Sharing under Apple's terms. We never receive your card or payment details. ChefLoop only reads Apple's confirmation, on your device, that the purchase is active. Nothing about your purchases is sent to us.

What ChefLoop never does

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting services or third-party code.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data by us.

Network use

ChefLoop connects to the internet only when you ask it to import a recipe from a web link. It then loads that page, and the main photo on it, directly from the website you chose. That website sees your request like any browser visit, and if the page is shown inside ChefLoop it may use its own cookies, as in a browser. ChefLoop sends no information about you to anyone else. The other network use is iCloud sync, handled by Apple.

On-device AI and voice

If your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence and you turned it on, ChefLoop may use Apple's on-device model to read recipes, sort items, and suggest short text. It runs on your phone. ChefLoop does not use cloud AI. Photos of printed recipes are read on your phone. Voice commands in cooking mode are understood on your phone and the audio is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera: to photograph your finished dish. Only when you tap Take Photo.
  • Photos: when you choose pictures, you pick them in Apple's picker. ChefLoop sees only the ones you select.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for hands-free commands in cooking mode, only while the microphone button is on.
  • Alarms: so cooking timers ring when the phone is locked or silent.

You can change any of these in Settings, ChefLoop.

Children

ChefLoop is a general cooking app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices and deleting data

You can export everything, and delete anything, inside the app. Deleting the app removes the data on the phone. To remove the copy in iCloud, see Support.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
