Support

Quick answers first. If you are still stuck, write to looptecklabs@gmail.com and say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.

How do I import a recipe?

Open the Recipes tab and tap the add button. You can paste a web link, choose photos of a printed recipe, paste text, or open a .cookloop file. You can also use Share, then ChefLoop, from Safari. Every imported recipe goes to a review screen first. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Some sites block automatic downloads. ChefLoop then shows the page itself so you can pass any check, and imports what is on screen.

Can I make recipes with an AI assistant?

Yes. The ChefLoop recipe skill (for AI assistants that support skills) formats a recipe so ChefLoop reads it exactly. Ask for the recipe "for ChefLoop", then open the file or paste the text into the app. Files must be in the current format. If ChefLoop says a file is too old, ask the assistant to generate it again.

How do I sync between my devices?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and keep iCloud turned on for ChefLoop (Settings, your name, iCloud, Apps Using iCloud). Syncing is automatic and uses your private iCloud space. A big first sync can take a few minutes.

What is a household, and how do I start one?

A household lets people who live together, each with their own iPhone and Apple Account, share recipes, the meal plan, the Shopping List and the Pantry. Open Settings, then Household, then Start Household. You become the owner. ChefLoop makes a backup of your data first. Sign in to iCloud first: sharing needs it.

How do I invite someone?

As the owner, open Settings, Household, Invite Someone, and send the link in Messages, by AirDrop or by copying it. The person opens the link on their iPhone, taps Join, and the household's recipes, plan, list and pantry appear. Only the owner can invite or remove people.

What happens to my own recipes when I join?

Nothing is deleted or uploaded. Your own recipes stay on your iPhone, private, under Mine in the Recipes tab. The household's recipes show under Household. In Settings, Household, My own recipes, you can add any of your recipes (or all) to the household: a copy is added and yours stay. While you are in a household, the Meal Planner, Shopping List and Pantry show the household's; your own come back when you leave.

How do I leave a household?

Settings, Household, Leave Household. Keep a copy of the recipes is on by default: the household's recipes are copied into your own recipes first. The household's meal plan, list and pantry are not copied. Your own list, plan and pantry come back.

Can the owner leave?

No. The owner can only stop sharing: everyone else loses access and each person is offered a copy of the recipes. Someone else can then start a new household and everyone can join it. If the owner deletes their iCloud data, the household's data is gone for everybody, so make a backup now and then (Settings, Household, Back Up Household).

I was removed, or the household ended. What happens to my recipes?

ChefLoop keeps a recent copy of the household's recipes on your iPhone. The next time you open it, it says "This household ended" and offers Add to My Recipes or Discard. Nothing is lost silently.

Do we need a household to use ChefLoop on two of our own devices?

No. Several devices signed in to the same Apple Account sync automatically (see "How do I sync between my devices?"). A household is for people with different Apple Accounts. If two people share one Apple Account they are one person to ChefLoop.

Will my timers ring on a locked phone?

Yes. ChefLoop timers use the system alarm service. They ring in silent mode and during Focus, and they show on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Allow alarms the first time ChefLoop asks. If you said no, turn it on in Settings, ChefLoop.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose ChefLoop. There is a shopping list widget (you can tick items off right on it) and a Today widget for planned meals. You can also add the Quick add control to the Control Center or Lock Screen.

Voice commands in cooking mode

Tap the microphone button in cooking mode and say next, back, start timer, or add minutes. Speech is understood on your iPhone and is not sent anywhere. Allow the microphone and speech recognition when asked.

How do I back up or export my recipes?

Settings, Export All Recipes. You get one .cookloop file with every recipe, plus a standard web-recipe file for each one. Save it to Files or share it. To restore, open the file in ChefLoop or use Import Backup in Settings. Photos are not included in the export yet.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a recipe, list item or plan entry in the app; ChefLoop always asks you to confirm. Deleting the app removes the data on that phone. Copies in iCloud stay until you remove them: Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, ChefLoop, Delete Data. We hold no copy on any server, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Everything works without it. When your iPhone supports it and it is turned on, ChefLoop uses it to read photos and pages that have no structured recipe, and to suggest short titles. You always review the result.
